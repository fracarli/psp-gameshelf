GameShelf 1.10 - N64 direct-launch candidate

This is a launcher-only update for the existing, tested 1.08 setup with the
official DaedalusX64 1.1.8 PSP emulator already present. It is not a complete
first-install package. The separate 1.09 single-file first installer currently
contains seven systems; N64 can join it after hardware validation.

On the existing card, the launcher and generated N64 entries were backed up
before installation. Emulator executables, ROM filenames and saves are retained.
The official Daedalus binary was verified byte-for-byte against upstream.

For another existing installation, first back up the complete GAMESHELF folder,
Daedalus settings/saves, old N64 entries and seplugins configuration. Replace
only PSP/GAME/GAMESHELF/EBOOT.PBP with this candidate's EBOOT.PBP. Keep all
previous runtimes, modules, BIOS and original ROM directories. The stable
computer updater does not accept 1.10 yet.

N64 ROM directory: PSP/GAME/DaedalusX64/Roms/
Uncompressed formats: .z64, .v64, .n64. Up to 32 games; no nested directories.
The launcher passes two NUL-separated arguments: emulator executable and full
original ROM path. It checks file existence, safe paths and supported extensions.
ROMs are read in place. Original Daedalus settings and save paths remain in use.

Open GameShelf and press X to regenerate entries, then X to return home.
The prepared existing card also has four generated N64 entries and covers.
After regeneration, Moonptrl.bin keeps its corrected Moon Patrol display name.

Test Super Mario 64 first. Check launch, controls, normal exit, in-game save
and save/reload. Then try Mario Tennis, Paper Mario and Super Smash Bros.
Game compatibility, speed and graphics are Daedalus capabilities and may vary;
a working launch adapter does not establish compatibility for every game.
N64 launch and saves remain unconfirmed on actual PSP hardware.

On the prepared card, the optional filter hides Daedalus after creating the
four game entries. Its executable and ROM/save directories remain intact.
If a direct launch fails, remove DaedalusX64 from seplugins/gclite_filter.txt
and reset VSH to show the emulator again. The prior filter is backed up.
Restore the complete backup to roll back the launcher and filter together.
Stable 1.08 remains archived under releases/v1/08 and tag v1.08.
